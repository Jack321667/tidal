Privacy Policy — Tidal Orbit

Last updated: 2026-07-15

Tidal Orbit ("the app") is a single-player arcade game. There are no accounts and no logins, and we do not build a profile of you. This policy explains the few cases where data does leave your device.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no data about you. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Gameplay itself works offline.

Data stored on your device

Most of what the app remembers stays on your device only: your high score, your coin balance, your unlocked orbitals, your purchased and selected orb skins, and your settings (sound, music, haptics, reduce motion). This data is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

If you make a purchase, the app uses RevenueCat to process and validate it with the app store. Your payment details are handled entirely by the app store and are never seen by us or by RevenueCat. To validate a purchase and restore it later, RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, a randomly generated app-user identifier that is not linked to your real identity, and basic device and country information. This happens only if you buy something or tap "Restore Purchases".

Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboard so it can be ranked against other players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed in to Game Center, no score is transmitted and the game is unaffected.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app itself collects nothing; the limited purchase and leaderboard data described above is handled by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
